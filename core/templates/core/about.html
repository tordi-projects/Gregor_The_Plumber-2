About Luke The Plumber

Family-run, Gas Safe registered plumbing and heating engineers, trading across England since 2009.

Our story

Straightforward Plumbing, Done Properly

Luke The Plumber was founded in London on a simple idea: plumbing and heating work should be honest, well-priced, and done right the first time. What started as a one-van operation has grown into a trusted team of Gas Safe registered engineers covering London and the Home Counties — but the values haven't changed.

Every engineer on our team is fully qualified, DBS-checked, and trained to the same high standard, so whoever turns up at your door, you know you're getting a proper job. We give upfront pricing before any work starts, and we back every job with a 12-month workmanship guarantee.
